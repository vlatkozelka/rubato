# Shipping Policy

_Last updated: 2026-03-22_

## Processing time

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days before shipping. Orders placed
after 2pm local time are processed the following business day.

## Shipping speeds

- **Standard**: 5-7 business days, $5.99, free on orders $75 and over
- **Expedited**: 2-3 business days, $14.99
- **Overnight**: next business day if ordered before 12pm, $29.99

## Order tracking

A tracking link is emailed once an order ships. Tracking updates can take up
to 24 hours to appear after the label is created.

## Delayed or lost packages

If a package shows no tracking movement for **5 business days** past the
expected delivery window, contact support to open a carrier investigation.
Confirmed lost packages are reshipped at no cost or refunded, customer's
choice.

## International shipping

We currently ship to the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom.
International orders may be subject to customs fees, which are the
responsibility of the customer and are not included at checkout.

## Address changes

Shipping addresses can be changed only before an order enters processing.
Once an order shows "processing" or later, the address cannot be changed and
the order must be cancelled and re-placed if the address was wrong.
