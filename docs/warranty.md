# Warranty

_Last updated: 2026-08-10_

## What warranty covers

Warranty covers manufacturing defects and premature failure under normal use:
broken zippers and seam failure on apparel and outerwear, non-functioning
electronics out of the box or shortly after, and structural failure on bags
and footwear. Warranty does not cover damage from misuse, normal wear over
time, or cosmetic issues that don't affect function.

**Opened software is not covered by warranty.** Software license cards and
digital-good redemption codes are final sale once opened, regardless of
defect — see the Return Policy document's Opened Software section. This is
the only product category excluded from warranty entirely.

## Warranty window

For every other category, items are covered by warranty for **90 days from
the delivery date**. This 90-day window is the single, authoritative defect
timeline for all covered items — it applies regardless of whether the item
would otherwise fall under the Return Policy's 30-day standard window or
14-day electronics window, since those two windows govern change-of-mind
returns only, not defects.

## Defective items reported within the first two weeks

If a defect is reported within the **first 14 days of delivery**, it is
resolved with an immediate replacement or refund, no repair attempt required,
since a defect this early points to a manufacturing fault rather than wear.
Defects reported after day 14 but within the 90-day window follow the normal
warranty process below (repair, replace, or refund at our discretion after
review).

## Filing a warranty claim

Warranty claims require a description of the defect and, where possible, a
photo. Claims are reviewed within 2 business days. There is no cost to the
customer for warranty repairs, replacements, or associated return shipping.

## Relationship to the return policy

Warranty claims are handled separately from standard returns and never count
against the return-abuse threshold described in the Return Policy document,
since the item was defective rather than returned by choice.
